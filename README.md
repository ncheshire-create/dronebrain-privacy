# DroneBrain: Part 107 Prep - Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026

DroneBrain is published by Zbyt Studios.

DroneBrain works fully offline. It does not collect, store on a server, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

Your quiz progress, streak, and mock exam history are saved only on your own device and never leave it. Deleting the app, or using Reset progress in the app, removes this data.

The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or any other device data.

DroneBrain is an independent study aid. Its questions are original practice questions and are not the actual FAA exam. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Federal Aviation Administration.

Children: because no data is collected, the app is safe for users of all ages.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
